Background

Researcher in Urban & Transportation Systems

I am a Research Fellow at the Graduate School of Engineering, Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering (Urban and Transportation Systems), Toyohashi University of Technology (TUT), Japan.

My research focuses on developing integrated land use–transport models, urban microsimulation frameworks, and accessibility-based evaluation methods to support evidence-based urban and transportation policy.

Current work includes Quality of Life (QOL) and Quality of Business (QOB) evaluation for air-front smart cities, digital service substitution in urban contexts, and multi-layer social network simulation.
